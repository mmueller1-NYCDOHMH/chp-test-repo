# Chronic Conditions

Chronic conditions are long-lasting health issues — including diabetes, heart disease, asthma, and obesity — that affect daily life and place ongoing demands on the health system.

## What's included

This section shows neighborhood-level indicators related to chronic disease prevalence, hospitalization, and related risk factors, alongside citywide comparisons.

## Why it matters

Chronic diseases are the leading cause of death and disability in New York City. Rates vary significantly across neighborhoods and are closely linked to social and environmental conditions including income, housing, and access to care.

## Data notes

Indicators are sourced from the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene. Hospitalization data reflects NYC inpatient discharge records. Prevalence estimates are drawn from the Community Health Survey.
