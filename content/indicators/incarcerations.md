This indicator measures the rate of jail admissions — residents sent to local NYC jails — and does not include state or federal prison sentences. The rate is calculated per 100,000 adults ages 16 and older using DOC admissions data and ACS population estimates. Incarceration is both a consequence and a driver of poor health: incarcerated individuals often have elevated rates of chronic illness, mental health conditions, and substance use disorders, and re-entry poses significant barriers to housing, employment, and care.
