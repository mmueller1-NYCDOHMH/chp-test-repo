# HIV and Hepatitis C

HIV and Hepatitis C are infectious diseases tracked closely at the neighborhood level. Both conditions have effective treatments and prevention strategies, and progress is meaningfully shaped by local context.

## What's included

Indicators in this section reflect prevalence, new diagnoses, and the public health response across NYC neighborhoods.

## Why it matters

New York City has made significant progress in reducing new HIV diagnoses and expanding access to treatment and prevention tools like PrEP. Hepatitis C, often undiagnosed, remains an important focus given effective cures now exist. Neighborhood-level data helps direct resources and outreach where they are needed most.

## Data notes

HIV data is sourced from the NYC HIV Surveillance Annual Report. Hepatitis C data is drawn from the NYC Hepatitis C Surveillance Report. Both are published by the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene.
