# Demographics

This section presents basic population characteristics for the selected community district.

## What's included

- **Population size** — total number of residents
- **Median age** — the midpoint age of the population
- **Trend data** — how these figures have changed over recent years

## Why it matters

Understanding who lives in a neighborhood is essential context for interpreting health outcomes. Communities with different age structures, household compositions, or population sizes often face different health challenges.

## Data notes

Population estimates are based on the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey (ACS) 5-year estimates. Figures represent the most recently available data year.
