# About Community Health Profiles

Community Health Profiles provide a snapshot of health and social conditions in New York City's 59 community districts.

## How to use this site

Use the neighborhood selector or map on the left to choose a community district. Each profile includes key health indicators, demographic data, and trend information.

## Data sources

All data is drawn from the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene's Environment and Health Data Portal. Indicators are updated regularly as new data becomes available.

## Questions or feedback?

Contact the NYC DOHMH Bureau of Epidemiology Services.
